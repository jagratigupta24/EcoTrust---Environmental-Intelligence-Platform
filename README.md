
# 🌿 EcoTrust – Environmental Intelligence Platform
### Trustworthy Industrial Pollution Monitoring Platform

<p align="center">
  **Making Industrial Pollution Data Transparent, Reliable & Actionable**
</p>

EcoTrust is a centralized environmental monitoring platform designed to improve the reliability and transparency of industrial pollution data. It helps pollution control authorities monitor industrial emissions and effluents, identify suspicious data patterns, and make informed decisions through real-time insights and data trust scoring.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://eco-trust-peach.vercel.app/

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Industrial pollution monitoring systems generate large amounts of emission and effluent data. However, fragmented data sources, missing readings, inconsistent values, and delayed detection of pollution spikes make effective environmental monitoring challenging.

EcoTrust addresses this challenge by bringing pollution monitoring, data reliability assessment, anomaly detection, and location-based insights into one centralized platform.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

EcoTrust – Green Stack provides a unified platform that aims to:

- Monitor industrial emissions and effluents against permissible limits.
- Detect abnormal pollution patterns and suspicious readings.
- Evaluate data reliability through a Data Trust Score.
- Provide location-based monitoring from State → District → Industry → Parameter.
- Generate alerts and maintain historical records for investigation.
- Support environmental decision-making through AI-powered assistance.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Real-Time Monitoring:** Track industrial emission and effluent parameters.
- **Smart Anomaly Detection:** Identify unusual pollution spikes, missing values, and suspicious data.
- **Data Trust Score:** Assess the reliability and consistency of pollution data.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Present environmental insights in an organized interface.
- **Location-Based Monitoring:** Navigate pollution data by state, district, and industry.
- **Alerts & Evidence:** Maintain historical readings and records for further investigation.
- **AI-Powered Assistance:** Proposed RAG and Agentic AI capabilities for regulatory insights and analysis.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

The proposed system follows a modular architecture:

1. **Data Integration:** Collect pollution data from CEMS/CEQMS and other sources.
2. **Backend Processing:** Process and manage incoming environmental data.
3. **Data Reliability:** Evaluate data quality and identify anomalies.
4. **Database Layer:** Store monitoring records and historical information.
5. **Frontend Dashboard:** Display insights, pollution levels, and alerts.
6. **AI Assistance:** Support regulatory analysis and intelligent insights.

## 🌍 Impact & Benefits

- Improves visibility into industrial pollution levels.
- Helps identify suspicious or unreliable monitoring data.
- Supports faster response to pollution-limit violations.
- Enables better prioritization of industrial inspections.
- Promotes transparent environmental compliance.
- Provides a scalable approach to environmental monitoring.
